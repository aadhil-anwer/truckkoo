1. Who we are

This website is operated by Truckkoo (“Truckkoo”, “we”, “us” or “our”), a trucking and logistics provider based in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. We are the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us:

  • WhatsApp / Phone: +968 7517 2824
  • Location: Muscat, Sultanate of Oman

2. Governing law

This policy is designed to align with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Sultanate of Oman, issued under Royal Decree No. 6/2022, and its Executive Regulations, together with any other applicable Omani laws and regulations governing the protection of personal data.

3. What personal data we collect

3.1 Information you give us

When you use the quote form or contact us, you may provide:

  • Pickup and delivery locations (cities);
  • A description of your goods and the truck type required;
  • Any information you choose to include when you message us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email (for example your name, company and contact details).

Our quote form does not send data directly to a server we control — when you submit it, the details are placed into a pre-filled WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us. WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms and is subject to its own privacy policy.

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, we and our analytics provider may automatically collect:

  • Your IP address and approximate (city-level) location;
  • Device, browser and operating-system information;
  • Pages visited, links and buttons clicked, scroll depth, referring source and the time and duration of your visit;
  • Information stored through cookies and similar technologies.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (provided by Google) to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes the usage data described above.

We have enabled Google Signals, which may associate visit information with data from signed-in Google users who have consented to such association, for analytics and ads-personalisation purposes. This may involve Google’s own information such as device, location and browsing data.

You can control or refuse cookies and analytics in several ways:

  • Adjust the cookie and privacy settings in your browser;
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on;
  • Manage ads personalisation at Google My Ad Center and review your data via Google My Activity.

5. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and prepare transportation quotes;
  • Provide, arrange and manage our trucking and logistics services;
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website;
  • Measure and analyse traffic and the effectiveness of our content;
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.

6. Legal basis and consent

Under the Omani Personal Data Protection Law, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent and/or where processing is necessary to respond to your request, perform a service you ask for, or comply with a legal obligation.

By using this website, submitting the quote form, or contacting us, you consent to the collection and use of your data as described in this policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 1; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the website and analyse traffic (for example Google);
  • Communication platforms you choose to use to reach us (for example WhatsApp / Meta);
  • Carriers, partners and sub-contractors strictly as needed to deliver a service you have requested;
  • Competent authorities where required by Omani law or to protect our legal rights.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers (such as Google and Meta) process data on servers located outside the Sultanate of Oman. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled in accordance with the requirements of the Omani Personal Data Protection Law and an adequate level of protection.

9. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy, to provide the services you request, and to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations. Analytics data is retained for the period configured in our Google Analytics account, after which it is aggregated or deleted.

10. Your rights

Subject to the Omani Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Withdraw your consent to processing;
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority in Oman (the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology).

11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites and services (such as WhatsApp and Instagram). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Significant changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the website after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact us

For any privacy-related request or question, reach us on WhatsApp or by phone at +968 7517 2824, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman.
