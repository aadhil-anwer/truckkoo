# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one funnel:

- **Households** moving homes, furniture or single items within Oman, or shipping personal effects across the GCC. Often on a phone, often Arabic-first, want a price and reassurance fast.
- **Businesses** — traders, contractors, manufacturers, project teams — needing local distribution, GCC cross-border road freight, customs clearance, project/heavy cargo or warehousing. Want to know the operator is capable and reachable.

Both reach the site from search, WhatsApp links or word of mouth, on mobile, and the job-to-be-done is the same: *decide Truckkoo is trustworthy and start a conversation in under a minute.* WhatsApp is the primary conversion, not a contact form.

## Product Purpose

Truckkoo is an Oman-based trucking and logistics company connecting Oman to the wider GCC: local trucking, cross-border road freight, customs clearance, FTL/LTL, project cargo and warehousing — loads from a single pallet to 40-ton trailers. The site exists to convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations and to signal "real operator, no brokers, no delays." Success = a quote request or call started.

## Brand Personality

Driven. Delivered. Trusted. Confident, plainspoken, operator-grade — the voice of a company that actually runs trucks, not a freight-forwarding middleman. Warm and human, not corporate; premium and credible, not cheap or aggressive. Bilingual by default (English + Arabic, full RTL), so the personality must read identically in both directions.

## Anti-references

- **Generic freight-forwarder template** — stock blue corporate, globe icons, handshake photos, "global supply chain solutions" boilerplate.
- **Loud discount/aggregator look** — badge soup, neon, urgency banners, coupon energy.
- **Cold enterprise SaaS** — sterile navy dashboards, stat-grid heroes, sterile gradients.

The committed orange (#f1551f) on asphalt-black + white identity is correct and must be preserved. The brief is execution, not reinvention: sharpen craft, add credibility, don't change the personality.

## Design Principles

1. **WhatsApp is the product.** Every surface drives toward starting a conversation; nothing competes with that path.
2. **Show the trucks.** A logistics brand earns trust through real photography — fleet, roads, ports, warehouses — not icon cards alone. Imagery is credibility.
3. **Bilingual parity.** Every decision must hold in Arabic/RTL as well as it does in English. No move that only works LTR.
4. **Operator confidence, not corporate gloss.** Plain, specific, capable. Specifics (tonnages, cities, "no brokers") beat adjectives.
5. **Mobile-first, fast, reachable.** Most visitors are on a phone with one thumb; speed and tap-reach are design, not afterthoughts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Already established: skip links, visible focus rings, ≥44px tap targets, reduced-motion alternatives, semantic landmarks. Must hold: ≥4.5:1 body/placeholder contrast (current form placeholders fail and need fixing), full RTL mirroring, and motion that degrades gracefully for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
